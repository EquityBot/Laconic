# Optional editorial scoring

Use only when the user requests a score. Rate each dimension from 1 to 10 and cite a supporting span. Label the numbers as subjective editorial judgements, not measured quality, factual validation or authorship probabilities. A score cannot override a failed factual preservation check.

| Dimension | 1 to 3 | 4 to 7 | 8 to 10 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Directness | Point buried in setup | Point sometimes delayed | Point arrives when the reader needs it |
| Specificity | Empty abstractions | Mixed detail and generalities | Available evidence supports the claims |
| Voice | Generic or inconsistent | Some distinctive passages | Consistent with the supplied voice |
| Rhythm | Repetitive or staged | Occasional awkward patterns | Pace suits the content |
| Density | Much removable filler | Some repetition | Each passage serves a purpose |

Report a score for each dimension and the weakest supported finding. Do not invent specifics or keep cutting clean writing to raise a score.
