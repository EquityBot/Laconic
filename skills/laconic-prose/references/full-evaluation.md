# Full Laconic evaluation

Apply this entire checklist to audits, long documents and edits to a human writer's draft. Follow the comparison and failure handling instructions in [eval.md](eval.md).

## Meaning and evidence

1. Does every added factual detail come from the supplied material or an authorised, identified source?
2. Did names, quantities, units, dates, quotations, sources and distinct substantive claims survive without alteration or omission?
3. Are negation, scope, exceptions, uncertainty, causality and obligations preserved? Check may/must, some/all, conditions, comparison bounds, before/after and simultaneous/total distinctions explicitly.
4. Are unsupported claims flagged rather than made more certain by removing their attribution or caveats?
5. Have code, commands, paths, URLs, identifiers, structured data and required verbatim wording remained exact unless their modification was authorised?

## Voice and judgement

1. Would the writer recognise their vocabulary, cadence, formality, humour, uncertainty and useful digressions?
2. Were already effective passages left alone, including a completely clean draft?
3. Is each edit justified by a contextual defect or requested locale or format change? Treat word lists, scanners, sentence lengths and punctuation counts as inspection aids, with legitimate uses protected.
4. Are precise terms, literal uses, useful passive constructions, informative contrasts, ordinary courtesy and deliberate fragments intact?
5. Did the edit avoid invented opinions, experiences, jokes or personality borrowed from a writing sample?
6. Is cutting proportional to the problem, without losing substance or forcing every paragraph into the same shape?

## Clarity and pattern repair

1. Does the opening give this reader useful content, retaining personal setup when it earns its place?
2. Were empty vocabulary, staged contrasts, throat clearing, faux insight setups, colon reveals and dramatic fragments repaired where they weakened the text?
3. Were unsupported significance, superficial analysis, vague attribution and redundant interpretive commentary removed or flagged without changing the supported claim?
4. Were synonym cycling, empty negative lists, staged questions and fake-profound endings repaired without destroying real distinctions or useful questions? Were stacked hedges, chatbot residue, signposting transitions and heading echoes removed where they add nothing?
5. Does each general statement explain, connect, instruct or contribute a claim? Does each remaining summary serve the format or reader?
6. Does the prose avoid repetitive sentence and paragraph shapes without forcing artificial variation? Reread repairs in context for newly introduced filler, forced slang, fake typos, staccato or altered meaning. Preserve those voice features when they belong to the source.
7. Do headings, lists, tables and emphasis help the reader, preserving accessibility and required templates?
8. Does all original and edited prose use Australian spelling and grammar unless the user explicitly requested another variety? Check forms such as organise, colour and centre, noun/verb distinctions such as licence/license and practice/practise, and inconsistent variants. Preserve exact quotations, code, identifiers, paths, URLs, product names and official titles. Retain established computing terms such as program. In detect mode, flag locale mismatches without changing the source.
9. Does original prose follow the user's punctuation requirements, with no em dashes, whilst preserving quotations and protected literals? Are set phrases open (open source, plain language), established compounds closed up (email, online) and temporary compound modifiers unhyphenated after their noun? Preserve fixed compounds, spelt-out numbers and prefixes whose hyphen changes the meaning. Replace empty hyphenated buzzwords with the supported claim.
10. In replies, did the edit use known reader context without assuming absent knowledge? Did self-contained documents retain their prerequisites, provenance, material risks and required verification?

## Mode and delivery

1. For detect mode, is the source unchanged, with quoted spans, contextual reasons, local suggestions and judgement calls distinguished from clear defects?
2. Does the output avoid authorship guesses, detector evasion promises and unsolicited or unsupported quality scores? If the user requested a score, does it follow [scoring.md](scoring.md) and remain a subjective editorial assessment?
3. For a standalone edit, is the full draft returned with brief changes unless text-only output was requested? For embedded or file use, does delivery follow the surrounding task?
4. Were clarification questions limited to information required for a useful result?
5. Were credentials, tokens, keys and private personal values replaced with placeholders in every quoted span?
